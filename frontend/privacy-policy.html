Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy describes how the Polypharm ADE website handles information when you browse its public pages or use the Clinical App.

Information you provide

If you create an account, the service handles your name, email address, and password credential to create and secure your account. Passwords are stored as protected password hashes. The service uses a session cookie to keep you signed in; the cookie is set to expire after a limited period. Account and password-reset emails may be sent when you use those features. New account notifications may also be sent to the site administrator.

Medicine names entered for FDA label lookup are sent to the service to retrieve available label information. Do not enter patient names or other identifying health information.

Information stored in your browser

The public medication list is kept in the active page session and is not saved to your account. Refreshing or leaving the page clears its entries. Your browser also necessarily processes technical data required to load pages and maintain a secure session.

Why information is used

Information is used to provide account access, password recovery, medicine-label lookup, security, and service operation. It is not used by this site to make a diagnosis or treatment decision.

Service providers and links

The website relies on hosting, backend, email, and public information services to operate. Requests to those services may be processed under their own terms and privacy policies. Links to external sources open sites that Polypharm ADE does not control.

Your choices

You can stop using the site, clear the public medicine list by refreshing or leaving its page, or use the account controls in the Clinical App to request account deletion. For privacy questions, contact the team through the Contact the Team page.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website changes. The date above indicates the latest posted revision.
