About the project

Gachoki Michael Muriuki created and leads the Polypharm ADE Support Project, a research prototype focused on helping healthcare professionals identify potentially harmful medicine combinations and prevent adverse drug events among older adults taking multiple medicines.

The project explores clinical decision support using pharmacy knowledge, medication-safety rules, and intelligent analysis. It is presented as a research prototype.
