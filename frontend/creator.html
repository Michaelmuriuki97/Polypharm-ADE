About the initiative

Gachoki Michael Muriuki created and leads Polypharm ADE, a research prototype focused on helping healthcare professionals identify potentially harmful medicine combinations and prevent adverse drug events among older adults taking multiple medicines.

This work explores clinical decision support using pharmacy knowledge, medication-safety rules, and intelligent analysis. It is presented as a research prototype.
