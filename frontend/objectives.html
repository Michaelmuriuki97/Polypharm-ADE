RESEARCH GOALS

Study Objectives

01

Assess Polypharmacy

Determine the prevalence of polypharmacy and adverse drug events among older adults.

02

Identify Medication Risks

Identify common drug interactions and potentially inappropriate medications.

03

Build Smart Support

Develop an AI or rule-based system capable of flagging potentially harmful medication combinations.

04

Pilot the System

Test the system using prescription data and evaluate its ability to identify higher-risk profiles.

05

Compare Performance

Compare automated system performance with manual pharmacist assessment.

06

Evaluate Usability

Assess healthcare professionals' perceptions, attitudes, and ease of using the system.

07

Scale the Solution

Develop practical recommendations for implementing the system across healthcare facilities.
