A SIMPLE SAFETY JOURNEY

Small steps can support safer medicine use

Use these three steps to prepare for a useful conversation about medicines.

1

Check

Read the medicine label and follow the written directions. Note anything that is unclear.

2

Ask

Talk with a pharmacist or clinician about questions, side effects, or how medicines fit together.

3

Act

Follow the guidance you receive, and contact a health professional if a concern remains.
