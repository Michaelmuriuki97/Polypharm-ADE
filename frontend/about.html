THE STUDY

About the Study

The purpose of this study is to design and evaluate a smart clinical support system capable of detecting and flagging potentially harmful drug combinations in real time.

The system is designed to support healthcare professionals in preventing adverse drug events among older adults aged 60 years and above who are receiving multiple medications.

The study focuses on healthcare facilities within the country and combines pharmaceutical knowledge, clinical decision support, data analysis, and intelligent automation.
