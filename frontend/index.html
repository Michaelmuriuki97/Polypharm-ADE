THE PROJECT

About the Study

The purpose of this study is to design and evaluate a smart clinical support system capable of detecting and flagging potentially harmful drug combinations in real time.

The system is designed to support healthcare professionals in preventing adverse drug events among elderly patients aged 60 years and above who are receiving multiple medications.

The project focuses on healthcare facilities within the country and combines pharmaceutical knowledge, clinical decision support, data analysis and intelligent automation.

RESEARCH GOALS

Project Objectives

01

Assess Polypharmacy

Determine the prevalence of polypharmacy and adverse drug events among elderly patients.

02

Identify Medication Risks

Identify common drug-drug interactions and potentially inappropriate medications.

03

Build Smart Support

Develop an AI or rule-based system capable of flagging harmful medication combinations.

04

Pilot the System

Test the system using prescription data and evaluate its ability to identify high-risk profiles.

05

Compare Performance

Compare automated system performance with manual pharmacist assessment.

06

Evaluate Usability

Assess healthcare professionals' perceptions, attitudes and ease of using the system.

07

Scale the Solution

Develop practical recommendations for implementing the system across country facilities.

03
RESEARCH PROCESS

Methodology

01

Retrospective Data Analysis

Analyse prescription and pharmacy records to identify medication patterns and potential adverse drug events.

02

Clinical Rule Development

Incorporate Beers Criteria, STOPP/START guidelines and drug interaction knowledge.

03

Intelligent System Development

Develop a smart clinical support engine capable of analysing medication profiles and generating risk alerts.

04

Pilot Testing

Test the system using appropriate patient prescription datasets.

05

Evaluation

Compare system results with pharmacist assessment and evaluate accuracy and usability.
