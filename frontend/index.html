Report a medicine problem

You can report a suspected side effect, medication error, or poor-quality health product to Kenya’s Pharmacy and Poisons Board. A suspected reaction can be reported without proving that a medicine caused it. This website does not submit reports on your behalf.

SUPPORTING SOMEONE YOU CARE ABOUT

Caregiver Corner

Before an appointment

Bring an up-to-date list of prescription medicines, non-prescription products, vitamins and supplements. Include the packages or photos of labels when practical.

During a medicine review

Ask what each medicine is for, how to follow its written directions, what effects to watch for, and whom to contact with concerns.

Keep the person involved

Invite the person you support to share their priorities and questions. Help record answers and follow-up steps with their agreement.

When something changes

Contact a pharmacist or clinician about confusing directions or suspected medicine problems. Do not change prescribed treatment on someone else's behalf.

EVIDENCE, PRIVACY & LIMITS

Use these tools with care

Educational summaries link to their sources. The local checker uses the existing Polypharm ADE research rules and has not been established here as a complete or independently validated clinical system. FDA label lookup remains in the separate authenticated clinical app. Automated output cannot diagnose a problem or replace individual advice from a qualified health professional.

The medicine list and checker run in your browser page session; this public feature does not save the entries, put them in a URL, or send them to the backend. Use “Clear my information” to remove entries immediately. A page refresh also clears them. Avoid entering names or other identifying information.

For concerning symptoms, contact a health professional. Severe or rapidly worsening symptoms need urgent assessment. Do not delay urgent care to complete a website tool.

HELP & GUIDANCE

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Polypharm ADE?

Polypharm ADE is a clinical support system and research prototype designed to help healthcare professionals identify potential medication-related risks and support safer care.

How does the FDA label lookup work?

It retrieves available FDA drug-label information and checks whether the medicines you entered are mentioned in the retrieved Drug Interactions text. A mention is a signal to review the label, not proof of a harmful interaction.

Does a result tell me whether two medicines are safe together?

No. The lookup may not identify every interaction and does not replace a complete clinical review. A pharmacist or prescriber should assess the medicines and the patient’s circumstances.

Should I enter patient names or identifying details?

No. Enter medicine names only and do not submit patient names or other identifying information.

How do I use the clinical app?

Open the Clinical App, add the generic or brand medicine names, and select “Look up FDA labels.” Review the summary first, then open “More information” to see the available label details.
