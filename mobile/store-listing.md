## Description

Polypharm ADE helps you prepare for safer conversations about medicines.

- Keep a personal list of medicine names in secure storage on your device.
- Share the list only when you choose, using your phone’s share sheet.
- Read practical Check, Ask, and Act guidance.
- View the latest summary of reports published by Kenya’s Pharmacy and Poisons Board and open the official source.
- Open the Polypharm ADE website for its sign-in based FDA drug-label lookup.

The app does not diagnose, prescribe, or determine whether medicines are safe together. Public report totals represent submissions, not every medicine error or a population risk rate. Confirm medicine questions and care decisions with a qualified pharmacist or prescriber. The FDA lookup displays available label information and is not a complete interaction analysis.
